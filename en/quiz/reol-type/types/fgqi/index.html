You stand in the front row without reason, becoming one with intense music through pure instinct. For you, live shows are meant to be "felt with your body."

Body Moves First

The moment the intro plays, your body reacts before you think. Asked why you love that song, you can only say "I don't know, but it's incredible" — and that's perfectly fine.

A World Beyond Words

Even after the show, words beyond "it was good" don't come easily. But you instinctively know that something is lost the moment you try to put it into words.

Solitude and Fulfillment in the Front Row

You tend to prefer going solo, free to become one with the music without worrying about others. The satisfaction of leaving the venue drenched in sweat is something only a Fighter understands.
