The Bijigaku Tour spans 21 cities across Japan from March to July. Many fans are planning to attend multiple shows. Here are some tips for planning your tour trips efficiently.

## Back-to-Back Shows

The Bijigaku Tour has 4 pairs of consecutive shows at nearby venues. Attending both is highly recommended.

#### 🐻 Hokkaido Set — 5/9 Asahikawa → 5/10 Sapporo

[map:Asahikawa → Sapporo Route](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Asahikawa+Station+to+Sapporo+Station&output=embed)

- Asahikawa to Sapporo is about 1 hour 25 minutes by JR limited express
  - You can move after the show and arrive in Sapporo the same day
  - By car, it's about 2 hours via the Dōō Expressway. Hokkaido is great for road trips
- Plenty of time to enjoy Asahiyama Zoo in Asahikawa and Genghis Khan BBQ & night views in Sapporo
- **2 nights / 3 days** including the night before is ideal
  - Accessible from either Asahikawa Airport or New Chitose Airport

#### 🏔 Kōshinetsu Set — 5/23 Nagano → 5/24 Niigata

[map:Nagano → Niigata Route](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Nagano+Station+to+Niigata+Station&output=embed)

- Nagano to Niigata is about 1 hour 10 minutes by Hokuriku Shinkansen
  - Note: you may need to transfer at Jōetsumyōkō Station
  - By car, about 2.5 hours via the Jōshinetsu and Hokuriku Expressways. The view from Myōkō SA is stunning
- A luxurious course: visit Zenkō-ji Temple → live show → fresh seafood from the Sea of Japan the next day

#### ⛩ Kyoto & Gifu Set — 5/30 Kyoto → 5/31 Gifu

[map:Kyoto → Gifu Route](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Kyoto+Station+to+Gifu+Station&output=embed)

- Kyoto to Gifu is about 1 hour 40 minutes by JR Tōkaidō Line
  - About 1 hour via Shinkansen (Kyoto → Nagoya → Gifu)
  - By car, about 1.5 hours via the Meishin Expressway — no need to repack your luggage
- Staying the night before is recommended if you want to explore Kyoto

#### 🌋 Kyushu Set — 4/18 Nagasaki → 4/19 Kumamoto

[map:Nagasaki → Kumamoto Route](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Nagasaki+Station+to+Kumamoto+Station&output=embed)

- Nagasaki to Kumamoto is about 2.5 hours via Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen + local train
  - Highway bus is about 3.5 hours at less than half the price
  - By car, about 2 hours via the Nagasaki and Kyushu Expressways. Consider a hot spring detour with a rental car
- Enjoy the ultimate gourmet relay: Nagasaki champon → Kumamoto horse sashimi

## Area-Based Trip Plans

Even for non-consecutive shows, grouping nearby dates and areas together is efficient.

#### Plan A: Shikoku & Chūgoku Region (March)

- 3/20 Tokushima → 3/22 Shūnan (Yamaguchi)
  - 2 days in between — stay in Tokushima for sightseeing, then travel the next day
  - Tokushima to Shūnan is about 4–5 hours by local trains
  - By car, about 4 hours via the Seto Ōhashi Bridge and Sanyō Expressway. The Shimanami Kaidō route is also recommended

#### Plan B: Kyushu Set (April)

- 4/18 Nagasaki → 4/19 Kumamoto
  - Back-to-back shows — **2 nights / 3 days** is the most efficient
  - By car, you have total freedom to move around Kyushu. Nagasaki to Kumamoto is about 2 hours
- Okinawa (4/29) is 10 days later, so plan it as a separate trip

#### Plan C: Chūbu Circuit (Late May)

- 5/23 Nagano → 5/24 Niigata → 5/30 Kyoto → 5/31 Gifu
  - Split into 2 trips, or do all 4 shows in one week-long journey
  - For the full circuit, fill the weekdays in between with sightseeing
  - By car, no luggage worries for the whole trip — just leave outfits for all 4 venues in the trunk

#### Plan D: Hall Shows Marathon (June–July)

- 6/12 Sendai → 6/19 Osaka → 6/27 Fukuoka → 7/4 Nagoya → 7/10 Tokyo
  - The second half is all hall shows — pick your favorite combinations
  - The Tokyo finale is a must — pair it with 1–2 more shows
  - Nagoya to Tokyo is about 3.5 hours by car — a viable option for nearby shows

## Choosing Your Transport

Pick your transport based on distance and budget.

#### ✈️ Flights

- **Recommended routes**
  - Okinawa, Hokkaido (Asahikawa & Sapporo), Kyushu (Nagasaki, Kumamoto, Fukuoka)
- Early-bird fares can be under ¥10,000 one way
- LCCs mainly depart from Narita and Kansai airports
  - Factor in travel time from the airport to the venue

#### 🚄 Shinkansen (Bullet Train)

- **Recommended routes**
  - From Tokyo: Utsunomiya, Sendai, Nagano, Niigata, Nagoya, Kyoto, Osaka
  - From Osaka: Kobe, Okayama area, Fukuoka
- Discounts available through EX-IC and Smart EX
- Highly reliable timing — easy to travel even after the show ends

#### 🚌 Highway Bus

- **Recommended routes**
  - Tokyo ↔ Sendai, Tokyo ↔ Nagano, Osaka ↔ Nagoya, Fukuoka ↔ Nagasaki/Kumamoto
- Overnight buses save on accommodation costs
  - However, taking an overnight bus after a live show is physically demanding — only if there's no show the next day
- 3-row independent seats make a huge difference in comfort

#### 🚗 Car

- Group trips can be the cheapest option when splitting costs
- Always check parking availability in advance
  - Parking info is listed on each venue's detail page
- For long distances, calculate highway tolls + gas costs before deciding

## Budget Estimates

Rough per-show costs when attending multiple shows.

#### Day Trip (Shinkansen Range)

- Transport: ¥5,000–15,000
- Meals: ¥2,000–3,000
- **Total: ¥7,000–18,000** + ticket price

#### 1-Night Trip (Shinkansen or Flight)

- Transport: ¥10,000–30,000
- Accommodation: ¥5,000–10,000
  - Capsule hotels can be as low as ¥3,000
- Meals: ¥3,000–5,000
- **Total: ¥18,000–45,000** + ticket price

#### 2+ Night Trip (Back-to-Back Show Sets)

- Transport: ¥15,000–40,000
- Accommodation: ¥10,000–20,000
- Meals: ¥5,000–10,000
- **Total: ¥30,000–70,000** + ticket price

### Money-Saving Tips

- **Book early-bird flights** as soon as they go on sale
- **Reserve hotels early** to secure business hotels
  - They tend to fill up quickly after the tour is announced
- **Overnight buses** save on both transport and accommodation
- **Back-to-back show sets** let you enjoy 2 shows for the cost of 1 trip

## Planning Your Schedule

#### Day Trips

- Aim to arrive 3 hours before the show starts
  - This gives you time to scout the venue area, secure a coin locker, and eat
- Always check the last train/bus after the show
  - Rural venues often have early last trains

#### Overnight Stays

- **Booking a hotel near the venue eliminates the need for coin lockers**
  - Check in, drop off your bags, and head to the venue hands-free
  - Save the hassle and cost of finding a coin locker
  - You can head straight back to the hotel after the show — no need to worry about the last train
- If sightseeing the next day, use a coin locker after checkout
- Book a mid-afternoon return trip so you're not rushed
